Good Questions

Before you enquire

How much does a deck or patio cost in Perth? +

Every project is different, so we never give a guessed number over the phone. After a free on-site visit we send a clear, itemised quote covering materials, labour and any approvals, so you know exactly where your money goes before anything starts.

How long will my build take? +

Most decks and patios run from a few days to a couple of weeks depending on size and complexity. We give you a realistic timeline in your quote and a confirmed start date, and we keep you posted the whole way through.

Composite or timber decking, which should I choose? +

Composite is the low-maintenance choice that stays looking new with almost no upkeep, ideal for busy families. Timber offers natural warmth and character but needs occasional oiling. We will walk you through the trade-offs for your space and budget so you can decide with confidence.

Do I need council approval? +

Some patios, pergolas and structures do require council or shire approval in WA. The good news is we handle it. We prepare the documentation and manage the application so you do not have to chase paperwork.

Is your work guaranteed? +

Yes. We are fully licensed and insured, and we stand behind our workmanship with a warranty. We also use quality materials backed by manufacturer warranties, and we will explain exactly what is covered before you commit.

Which areas of Perth do you service? +

We work right across the Perth metro area, from the coast to the foothills. If you are unsure whether we cover your suburb, just ask in your enquiry and we will let you know straight away.
